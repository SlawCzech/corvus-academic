## 1. Required Structure

The thesis MUST contain ALL of the following elements (in this order):

### 1.1 Title Page
### 1.2 Independence Statement
### 1.3 Table of Contents

### 1.4 Introduction (Wstęp) — CRITICAL ELEMENT
Must contain ALL of the following:
- [ ] Research problem definition (theoretical and/or empirical)
- [ ] Justification of significance and topicality
- [ ] Research objectives (main and/or specific)
- [ ] Research question(s) OR research hypothesis/hypotheses
- [ ] Scope definition: subject, object, time, geographic
- [ ] Description of research methods, techniques, and tools used
- [ ] Description of thesis structure
- [ ] General characterization of literature used

### 1.5 Main Text
- Organized into chapters and subchapters
- Must include: literature review and academic discussion
- Empirical/project theses: description of research methods,
  tools, and solution description

### 1.6 Conclusions (Zakończenie) — CRITICAL ELEMENT
Must contain ALL of the following:
- [ ] Response to the research problem stated in the introduction
- [ ] Reference to research objective(s)
- [ ] Reference to hypothesis/hypotheses (confirmation or rejection)
- [ ] Concluding remarks summarizing research results

### 1.7 Bibliography (Wykaz źródeł)
- APA style, alphabetical order, numbered

### 1.8 Lists
- List of figures (if applicable)
- List of tables (if applicable)
- List of attachments (if applicable)

### 1.9 Attachments (optional)
- Non-integral materials must be in electronic form

---

## 2. Formal and Technical Requirements

### 2.1 Length
- **Required:** 80,000 – 130,000 characters including spaces
- Attachments and research tools NOT counted toward this limit
- Project theses: theoretical chapter may be shorter in favor of
  solution description

### 2.2 Bibliography Requirements
- **Minimum:** 25 bibliographic sources
- **Minimum:** 4 foreign-language sources
- Netography (websites) does NOT count toward the 25-source minimum
- Project theses: source count may be reduced in favor of
  project-specific materials

### 2.3 Citation Style
- **Required:** APA style throughout
- All citations must enable identification of the source
- Bibliography: alphabetical order, numbered

### 2.4 Language Quality
- Linguistically correct: spelling, grammar, style, punctuation
- Serious language or formal errors may result in supervisor
  refusing to verify the thesis

---

## 3. Academic Competencies to Assess

The thesis must confirm the student's ability to:

- Independently select and evaluate source materials
- Analyze and synthesize theoretical achievements in the discipline
- Apply research methodology appropriate to the problem
- Conduct logical argumentation
- Draw and formulate conclusions from evidence
- Use clear and precise academic language

---

## 4. Severity Classification for Corvus Analysis

### CRITICAL — blocks admission to defense
- Missing required structure element (introduction, conclusions,
  bibliography, title page, independence statement)
- Introduction missing: research problem, objectives, or hypotheses
- Conclusions missing: response to research problem or hypotheses
- Fewer than 25 bibliographic sources (excluding netography)
- Fewer than 4 foreign-language sources
- No in-text citations present (plagiarism risk)
- Length significantly below 80,000 characters
- APA citation style not used

### MAJOR — significant deficiency requiring correction
- Introduction incomplete (missing one or more required elements)
- Conclusions incomplete (missing one or more required elements)
- Literature not current or not appropriate for research problem
- Bibliography borderline: 25–30 sources of questionable quality
- Significant language errors (grammar, spelling, style)
- Missing lists of figures/tables when figures/tables are present
- Team thesis missing clear authorship attribution per section

### MINOR — improvement recommended
- Minor language or style issues
- Bibliography formatting inconsistencies (APA minor deviations)
- Chapter length proportions suboptimal but acceptable
- Heavy use of netography (not critical, but noted)
- Attachments not in electronic form (project theses)
