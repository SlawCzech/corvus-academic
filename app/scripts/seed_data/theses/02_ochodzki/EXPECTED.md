# Ryszard Ochódzki — expected findings

BA, average. Rubric: `bachelor.md` (UE Katowice, Zarządzenie 117/22 zał. 1).
~81 000 chars as extracted (tables included).

Formally almost in order — every required element is there, the language is
correct, the literature count passes. The problem is substance: a club president
describes his own club, declarations stand in for data, and the conclusions go
far beyond the material. **No CRITICAL finding is expected**; a review that
fails the thesis on formal grounds is wrong, and so is one that takes its
claims at face value.

## MAJOR (rubric § 5)

1. **Introduction incomplete**: no time or spatial scope, methods in one
   sentence, no characterisation of the literature. The hypothesis ("model jest
   efektywny i nowoczesny") has no criteria — it cannot be falsified.
2. **Conclusion incomplete and overreaching**: no limitations; "dowiodły, że
   model … jest najlepszym rozwiązaniem", "gwarantuje stabilność", "istotny wkład
   w naukę" — from one case.
3. **Literature of doubtful fit**: 31 items, but mostly general management
   classics and textbooks (Fayol, Taylor, Maslow, Griffin, Kotler…); about eight
   on sport, one of them a popular book (*Soccernomics*); nothing after 2018.
   Vague web sourcing: "(www.msit.gov.pl)", "Jak podaje Wikipedia", "według
   informacji dostępnych w internecie", "jak powszechnie wiadomo".

## MINOR

4. Bibliography in two formats (entries 2, 13, 27 in the old Polish style);
   "(Griffin)" without a year; several works invoked by name only.
5. Chapters 1–2 (theory) are about half the thesis and are not used in the
   empirical part; chapter 1 is a textbook summary.
6. Large netography of low quality ("Informacje prasowe i internetowe … (różne
   strony)").

## Substance: the tables contradict the text

7. "Systematyczny rozwój bazy członkowskiej" — table 1: 412 → 438 → 365 members
   (−11% over the period, −17% from the 2021 peak).
8. "Wszystkie źródła przychodów rozwijają się dynamicznie" — table 2: member
   fees 260 → 241; "dywersyfikacja" while the subsidy share rises 48% → 61%;
   sponsors 9 → 5, three of five through the president's personal relations.
9. Revenue "+ blisko 40%" is nominal; consumer prices rose about as much over
   2020–2024, so real revenue is flat. Costs and the financial result are
   withheld as "tajemnica klubu" in the same paragraph that calls the finances
   "w pełni transparentne".
10. "Stabilne, wysokie wyniki sportowe" — table 4: relegation from IV liga in
    2022/23 (renamed "restrukturyzacja sportowa"), athletics medals 7 → 2;
    failures blamed on referees and injuries.
11. Youth training is "najważniejszy priorytet" — table 3: 8% of spending, less
    than the board's foreign trips (11%); two home-grown players in five years;
    youth coaches paid months late, three resigned.

## Method

12. **The author evaluates his own model.** Six interviewees are his
    subordinates, interviewed by him, in his office, not anonymously, with
    leading questions ("Jakie są … najmocniejsze strony modelu?"). Result:
    "100% badanych".
13. Inconvenient answers are discarded: R4 (equipment, unpaid coaches) —
    "kwestie operacyjne … nie zostały uwzględnione"; R6 (publish finances, call
    member meetings) — "zostaną rozważone w odpowiednim czasie".
14. "Expert" rating of the pillars by the author himself: 4.9 / 5.
15. SWOT with eight strengths and two pseudo-weaknesses, both about how others
    perceive the club. SWOT is credited to Porter — a factual error.
16. Comparison with other clubs rests on "według wiedzy autora" and "nie ulega
    wątpliwości"; no data.
17. Herzberg invoked to justify motivating staff with diplomas instead of pay.

## Governance red flags presented as strengths

A good review notices these although the thesis frames them positively:
no general meeting since 2021, 2022–2024 financial statements not approved by
members; two board meetings a year; every expense above 500 zł approved by the
president; the audit committee "raised no remarks"; the district office queried
the 2023 subsidy settlement; four board trips a year to London without a written
partnership agreement; the president's office renovated while the changing rooms
wait; most of the 2023 marketing budget spent on an oversized bear mascot.
The 2025–2029 strategy (600 members, promotion to III liga) runs against every
trend in the tables and has no schedule or budget.
