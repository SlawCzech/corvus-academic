# Jan Paweł Adamczewski — expected findings

MA in article form, ambitious and overreaching. Rubric: `master_article.md`
(UE Katowice, Zarządzenie 117/22 zał. 2). Body ~53 500 chars, whole document
~62 000.

Kargul has a wrong method and honest intent; **Adamczewski has the thesis
decided before the research.** The text is fluent, confident and full of real
literature — a model that mistakes confidence for quality will rate it highly.

## CRITICAL (rubric § 4)

1. **Abstract of 225 words** (limit 150).
2. **Eight keywords** (3–5 allowed), two of them the author's own name and
   village.
3. **Conclusions without real limitations**: "autor nie dostrzega takich, które
   mogłyby wpłynąć na wnioski"; the only one named is blamed on the archives.

## MAJOR / MINOR

4. Literature at the edge of the minimum: 43 items, one of them the author's own
   unpublished typescript, two of them 16th–17th-century treatises.
5. Discussion "argues" with earlier work by dismissal rather than argument.
6. Body slightly under the suggested 55 000 chars.
7. Self-citation of an unpublished manuscript (Adamczewski, 2025) as proof
   ("jak wykazałem…"), three times.

## The aim is out of proportion to the material

8. "Optymalny model … niezależnie od epoki" (H3) from five years of one manor's
   accounts. A "theorem" named after the author, said to close transaction-cost
   theory; the single case defended with Newton and gravity (4.5).
9. The case is declared "w pełni reprezentatywny" without any argument, and
   further cases are said to add "nothing but repetition".

## Confirmation bias

10. **Critical literature is missing or waved away.** Kula's *Teoria
    ekonomiczna ustroju feudalnego* — the standard work on exactly this
    calculation — is absent. So are the critiques of the authors he relies on
    (Fenoaltea on North & Thomas; the *Time on the Cross* controversy) and the
    modern institutional work on serfdom (Ogilvie, Dennison). Brenner and
    Acemoglu & Robinson are "ideological"; Leszczyński is "publicystyka";
    Markevich & Zhuravskaya "do not apply"; Smith "never ran a manor".
11. **Sources are read one way.** Domar explains why serfdom arises, not that it
    is efficient. Acemoglu & Wolitzky show coercion raises effort *and* lowers
    welfare — only the first half is quoted. North & Thomas limit their claim to
    the Middle Ages — he "corrects" them. Cerman, Hagen and Guzowski are bent
    into support. Małowist and Wallerstein are turned upside down.
12. **Evidence against the thesis is in the notes under the tables** and
    explained away:
    - rye yield on peasant plots 4.0–4.4 vs the manor's 3.0–4.1 (note to table 1);
    - the neighbour's manor, with two days of corvée and partial rents, yields
      4.6 and gains households — "nie zasługuje na zaufanie", comparison refused
      on three pretexts (3.6);
    - the daughter's letter criticising the corvée is excluded as "emocjonalny"
      (2.3).
13. **The tables show decline, the text reports success**: corvée 3 → 5 days,
    sowing 120 → 140, harvest 492 → 420, yield 4.1 → 3.0; grain sales
    1 240 → 870; settled households 23 → 17, fifteen runaways, three of them to
    the neighbour. Called "stabilny poziom", "elastyczność", "naturalna rotacja"
    and "samoczynna selekcja kadr". Rising tavern income (compulsory purchases by
    the peasants) is "dywersyfikacja".

## Method

14. **Rate of return of 504%** comes from pricing serf labour and land at zero
    ("kosztem jest to, za co się płaci"); pricing labour is rejected in advance
    as "sztuczne".
15. **Multi-criteria score 4.8** — criteria, weights and marks all set by the
    author alone, who declined to consult anyone because no comparable
    specialist could be found.
16. **Single source written by the interested party** (the landlord's own
    ledger), declared beyond doubt because he "had no reason to mislead
    himself"; no external verification; 31 disputes "all decided in the manor's
    favour" by the owner as judge are counted as low enforcement costs.
17. The author is the landlord's descendant and namesake — presented as an
    asset (2.6).
18. Table 3 compares transaction costs by assertion, not measurement.

## Recommendations

19. Policy conclusions for today: farm labour contracts the worker cannot
    terminate, housing tied to the duty to work, pay in kind, CAP rebuilt
    around "control over labour"; extension to construction and catering.
    A review should say plainly that these do not follow from the study and
    describe unfree labour.
